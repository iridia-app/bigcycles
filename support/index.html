❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What Pro options are available?

Iridia Pro is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or a one-time Lifetime purchase with no recurring charges. Open the app and tap "Iridia Pro" in Settings to see current prices.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Iridia, and tap "Cancel Subscription". Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid being charged. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that was used for the original purchase.

I purchased Pro but features are not unlocked. What should I do?

Try tapping "Restore Purchases" in Settings. If the issue persists, restart the app or reinstall it. If you still have problems, contact us at bigcycles.app@gmail.com.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why does Iridia ask for camera access?

The front TrueDepth camera is used to follow your gaze and blinks during exercises and to warn you when you hold the screen too close. Everything is processed in real time on your device; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Camera access is optional — you can do all exercises without it.

Eye tracking doesn't seem to work. What can I do?

Face tracking requires a device with a TrueDepth (Face ID) camera. Make sure camera access is allowed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera, your face is well lit, and the device is about an arm's length away.

Can Iridia diagnose eye problems?

No. The screening tests are for information only and are not a medical diagnosis. If a test shows something unusual, or you notice any change in your vision, please see an eye care professional.

How does Apple Health integration work?

If you allow it, completed exercise sessions are saved to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. You can change this at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Iridia.

How do I sync my progress between devices?

Sign in with the same Apple ID on all devices and make sure iCloud is enabled for Iridia (Settings → your name → iCloud). Your streaks, history and settings will sync automatically.

Can I use Iridia offline?

Yes. All exercises, the break timer and screening tests work offline. An internet connection is only needed for purchases and iCloud sync.

Which devices are supported?

Iridia works on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. The Watch app gives gentle break reminders and guided exercises right on your wrist.

Does the app collect any personal data?

No. Iridia does not collect or share any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for details.
