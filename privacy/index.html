Privacy Policy

Iridia — Last updated: September 2026

📋 Overview

Iridia is an eye-care app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch developed by Big Cycles. It offers eye exercises, a 20-20-20 break timer and vision screening tests. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app uses and what we do not collect.

📦 Data We Collect

Iridia does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. We do not operate servers that receive your data, and no account is required. All core features work offline.

📱 Data Stored on Your Device

Your exercise history, streaks, screening test results, reminders and settings are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud sync or choose to export a report yourself.

☁️ iCloud Sync

If iCloud is enabled on your device, Iridia may sync your progress and settings between your devices using your personal iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). This data is stored by Apple under your Apple ID and is not accessible to Big Cycles. You can turn off iCloud sync at any time in your device's Settings.

📷 Camera & Face Tracking

With your permission, Iridia uses the front TrueDepth camera (ARKit face tracking) to follow your gaze direction and blinks during exercises, and to estimate the distance between your face and the screen. This processing happens in real time, entirely on your device. No photos, video, or face data are recorded, saved, or transmitted. You can deny or revoke camera access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera; exercises remain available without tracking.

❤️ Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Iridia can write completed exercise sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. Iridia does not read other health data. Information shared with HealthKit is never used for advertising or marketing, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties. You can manage these permissions at any time in the Health app.

📄 Reports & Exports

You can generate a PDF report of your screening results, for example to show your eye doctor. The report is created on your device and is shared only when you choose to send it yourself.

📊 Analytics & Crash Reports

We do not collect any analytics, crash reports, or usage data from your device. We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

💳 Purchases

Subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information. Purchase records are managed by Apple under their privacy policy.

🔒 Cookies & Tracking

The app does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or any tracking technologies. This website does not use cookies either.

👶 Children

Iridia does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
