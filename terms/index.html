👁️ About the App

Iridia is a wellness app that helps you take regular screen breaks (20-20-20 rule), perform eye exercises, and run simple vision screening tests such as color vision tests, the Amsler grid, contrast and astigmatism checks.

⚕️ Medical Disclaimer

Iridia is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Screening tests are for informational purposes only and cannot replace an examination by a qualified eye care professional. Results may be affected by screen settings, lighting and viewing distance. If you notice any change in your vision, pain, flashes, floaters, or distortion, contact an eye doctor promptly. Stop any exercise that causes discomfort. If you have an eye condition or have had eye surgery, consult your doctor before doing eye exercises.
